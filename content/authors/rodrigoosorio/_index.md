Rodrigo Osorio Lépez is a Biomedical Engineer with an MSc and DSc in Engineering Sciences, specializing in Electrical Engineering from the University of Concepción. He currently serves as an Assistant Professor in Biomedical Engineering at the Universidad de Santiago de Chile (USACH). His work focuses on the intersection of engineering and data analysis, with a strong emphasis on neurostimulation and medical devices. His research includes the development of computational models for nervous system stimulation, optimizing functional electrical stimulation (FES), and characterization of interferential current stimulation. Previously, he was a founding partner of CapsSens SpA and a consultant at Scientia, highlighting his commitment to innovation in medical technologies.
